# Illustrative pumping station flood preparation

## Standby capacity review
For a water pumping station exposed to flooding, review recorded standby capacity with the water operations supervisor. Use review_standby_capacity to verify whether a backup arrangement is available and current. Do not infer that standby equipment is usable merely because an asset record says it exists. Confirm unresolved maintenance items and link existing field work.

## Access confirmation
Flooding can invalidate recorded access information. Ask the field operations team to confirm site access using verify_access. The supervisor decides whether any site visit is permitted. The assistant cannot declare a route safe or dispatch staff. Treat unconfirmed access as missing information and retain the original field report.
