# Illustrative work governance

## Human approval
AI outputs are draft recommendations. A human reviews the source evidence, installation context and existing work before selecting an allowed action, assignee and deadline. If the underlying alert changed after drafting, generate and review a fresh plan. Record the approving actor and timestamp. The demo actor field is not verified enterprise identity.

## Completion and closure
Move work from pending to in progress and then completed. Completion requires an outcome entered by the reviewer. Completing a task does not prove a weather threat has ended. Close an alert only after its linked work is complete and an operator records a reason. Retain the decision history.
