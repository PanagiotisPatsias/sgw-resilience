# Illustrative leadership reporting

## Situation briefing
A leadership briefing summarizes the latest warning revisions, priority assets, active alerts, outstanding tasks, owners and deadlines. Include an as-of timestamp and distinguish completed work from pending work. State data gaps, expired warnings and synthetic-data limitations. Base operational claims only on the supplied current snapshot.

## Evidence and uncertainty
Reference the source policies and retain the snapshot used to generate the briefing. Do not invent financial savings, avoided outages, diagnoses, future failures or response completion. If information is insufficient, identify what is missing. A briefing does not authorize new operational work.
