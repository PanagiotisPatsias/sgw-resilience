# Illustrative electrical asset escalation

## Qualified review
For an electrical substation with high flood exposure, notify the authorized grid operations lead through the existing workflow. Propose verify_flood_defences or verify_access only as reviews by qualified personnel. Switching, isolation and equipment control are outside this platform's action catalog and require separate authorization under real approved procedures.

## Open maintenance findings
Check whether a flood barrier or site readiness inspection is already open. Link the existing task rather than creating duplicate work. If the record is stale, use confirm_missing_data and ask the responsible engineer to verify it. A high priority index is not a probability of electrical failure.
