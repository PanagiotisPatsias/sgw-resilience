# Illustrative flood readiness policy

This document is fictional demo material, not an approved operating procedure.

## Warning verification
When a flood warning is received, verify its source, issue timestamp, geographic area and validity period. Distinguish the provider's warning severity from the utility's asset priority. A warning does not establish that an individual asset will flood. Review missing exposure information with the responsible engineer. Use the confirm_missing_data action when site information is missing or stale.

## Flood defence inspection
For exposed critical assets with an overdue flood defence inspection, propose verify_flood_defences. An authorized site team should verify the inspection status and report readiness under its own approved procedures. The platform must not direct entry into flooded or energized areas. Record the task owner and deadline after human approval. Check for an existing inspection task before proposing duplicate work.

## Changing warning areas
When a warning expands, reassess assets newly inside its geographic area. When the warning shrinks or expires, flag the change for operator review. Do not automatically close outstanding work or declare the site safe. Keep prior warning revisions and decisions for traceability.
